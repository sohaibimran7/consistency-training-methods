# Reproducing the paper

This consolidation is the integration authority for paper-producing code. Historical results and corrected reruns are distinct products; reproducing a flawed historical result does not validate it.

Implementation and inventory are under active integration. Do not launch training from this branch until the checked-in preflight and owner reviews pass.

## Documentation design

The [Document your code](https://goodresearch.dev/docs) and [Document your project](https://goodresearch.dev/pipelines) lessons were read on 28 September 2026. This workflow applies their guidance through descriptive command-line interfaces, explicit validation errors, tested contracts, versioned pipeline commands, and per-figure provenance. Installation, artifact access and failure recovery belong alongside the commands, not in an external chat.
